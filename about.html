THE NAME

Check the gate.

On a film set, once a shot is done, the AC inspects the camera gate for a stray hair or speck before the crew moves on. If it's clean, you've got the take. If it isn't, you go again. It's a small habit that decides whether the work holds up. We named the company after it.

A small crew that stays on the film from first call to final delivery.

We started as two people with one camera and a rule: don't hand over anything you wouldn't put your own name on. That's still how it works. We keep the slate small on purpose, run lean crews, and do our own post so the last look before delivery is genuinely ours.

The result is fewer projects a year, each one finished properly — brand films, documentaries, and commercials for people who care how the thing actually turns out.
